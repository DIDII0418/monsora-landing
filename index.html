How it works

From scattered work to the right context for what comes next.

Capture what matters, organize it into a Capsule, call up the context you need, and continue from where you left off.

01Capture

Bring scattered assets back into one place.

Bring images, video, prompts, references, scripts, audio, and outputs back from the web, your creative tools, or your computer.

02Capsule

Let AI organize what matters into reusable context.

Monsora understands related assets and organizes them into editable Capsules, keeping their available context connected.

03Call

Start from the right context, not from zero.

Find the asset or Capsule you need, then drag it into a creative tool — or make it callable by authorized AI agents through Monsora CLI.

04Continue

Keep creating from what came before.

Bring selected results back to Monsora, where they can be confirmed and added to the relevant Capsule — so each new result can become context for what comes next.

Why now

Files everywhere.
Context nowhere.

AI lets every idea take more forms, across more tools, models, and versions. References live in tabs, prompts in chats, outputs across tools, and versions in download folders. The work may remain, but the context that shaped it often disappears.
