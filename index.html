How it works

From scattered work to the right context for what comes next.

Capture what matters, organize it into a Capsule, call up the context you need, and continue from where you left off.

Capture

Bring scattered assets back into one place.

Bring images, video, prompts, references, scripts, audio, and outputs back from the web, your creative tools, or your computer.

Capsule

Let AI organize what matters into reusable context.

Monsora understands related assets and organizes them into editable Capsules, keeping their available context connected.

Call

Start from the right context, not from zero.

Find the asset or Capsule you need, then drag it into a creative tool — or make it callable by authorized AI agents through Monsora CLI.

Continue

Keep creating from what came before.

Bring selected results back to Monsora, where they can be confirmed and added to the relevant Capsule — so each new result can become context for what comes next.

Trust & control

Your assets stay yours.

Monsora is built for creators who care about ownership, privacy, and control. AI can suggest structure, while you can review and adjust how your assets are organized. You decide when generated results return to Monsora and what is made available to collaborators or AI agents.

Monsora preserves source information and creative relationships where they are available, without inventing what is missing. Third-party timestamps support traceability by recording when assets enter Monsora and how the work develops; they do not determine ownership.

Ownership, preserved

Source information and creative relationships stay intact where they exist — never invented.

You review the structure

AI suggests how assets are organized; you can always review and adjust it.

Access, on your terms

You decide what's shared with collaborators or AI agents — and when.

The future

Models change. Your creative world should stay with you.

Monsora starts with your personal creative memory. Over time, that memory can make the creative path itself searchable — not only what was made, but what changed, when, and why. Capsules can also support creative decisions, collaboration, and AI agents — with clear sources, permissions, status, and control. The goal is to preserve not only the result, but the creative path behind it.
